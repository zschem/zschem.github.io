Adapted Step 1 Procedure
  1. Reagent Preparation (Target Mn = 10.61 kDa, Feed Ratio 75 : 25 : 1): Combine 3.6608 g of styrene, 1.1489 g of maleic anhydride (MAN), and 0.1643 g (164.3 mg) of the chain transfer agent PADTC in a vial. Dissolve the mixture completely in 4.9969 g (~4.84 mL) of dry 1,4-dioxane, then add 0.0229 g (22.9 mg) of the initiator (AHCN) and dissolve thoroughly.
  2. Reaction Setup: Transfer the reaction solution into a round-bottom flask equipped with a magnetic stir bar. Collect and set aside a small aliquot (~50 μL) of the unreacted solution as a baseline (t = 0) for 1H NMR conversion tracking, then seal the flask with a rubber septum.
  3. Degassing & Polymerization: Purge the sealed flask by bubbling nitrogen gas (N2) through the solution for 15 minutes to remove dissolved oxygen. Heat the reaction mixture at 90 °C with continuous stirring for 24 hours.
  4. Conversion Check: Analyze a small aliquot of the heated mixture via 1H NMR against the initial baseline aliquot to confirm that monomer conversion reaches ≥80% (typically ~100% MAN and ~88% styrene conversion) before stopping the reaction.
  5. Purification & Isolation: Allow to cool, redissolve/dilute the crude polymer in minimal tetrahydrofuran (THF), and precipitate into a large excess of cold hexanes. Repeat this THF/hexane precipitation process two more times (3 total precipitations) to thoroughly remove unreacted monomers and residual solvent. Dry under vacuum to isolate approximately 3.7477 g of the target copolymer as a yellow powder.

Adapted Step 2 Procedure
  1. Reaction Mixture Preparation: Dissolve 1.8198 g of the yellow SMA copolymer in 11.2165 g (~10.86 mL) of 1,4-dioxane in a round-bottom flask. Add 0.9843 g (984.3 mg) of benzoyl peroxide (BPO) and mix until fully homogeneous.
  2. Degassing Setup: Seal the round-bottom flask securely with a rubber stopper. Deoxygenate the mixture by bubbling nitrogen gas (N2) through the solution for 15 minutes. Leave a vent/escape needle inserted through the stopper to prevent pressure buildup.
  3. Thermolysis / End-Group Cleavage: Heat the reaction flask at 82 °C with continuous stirring for 5 hours to thermally decompose BPO and cleave the yellow thiocarbonylthio (PADTC) RAFT end-groups.
  4. Purification & Isolation: Allow the reaction to cool to room temperature, then dilute/redissolve the polymer residue in minimal THF. Precipitate the solution into a large excess of cold hexanes to remove unreacted peroxide, benzoic acid byproducts, and cleaved fragments. Repeat the THF/hexane precipitation once more (2 total precipitations). Collect and dry the product to obtain a white to off-white powder (confirming removal of the yellow trithiocarbonate group).

Adapted Step 3 Procedure
  1. Dissolution: Dissolve 0.3000 g of the purified white SMA polymer in a minimal amount of THF (~1–2 mL) in a reaction vial or flask.
  2. Alkaline Addition: Add 5.00 mL of deionized water together with 0.84 mL (838 μL) of 4 M NaOH solution (equivalent to 0.1341 g solid NaOH). The polymer will initially form a cloudy suspension.
  3. Heating & Ring-Opening: Heat the reaction mixture at 55 °C with vigorous stirring overnight until the cloudy polymer suspension clarifies into a transparent, homogeneous solution.
  4. Clarification Check: If the mixture is not yet completely clear after overnight heating, add a few more milliliters of deionized water and continue stirring at 55 °C for an additional 5 hours until fully transparent.

Step 4 Procedure: Dialysis, Freeze-Drying & Nanodisc Assembly
  1. Dialysis: Transfer the clear hydrolyzed SMA solution from Step 3 into an appropriate dialysis bag and dialyze thoroughly against deionized water (changing the outer water bath 3–4 times) to remove excess NaOH and salts.
  2. Freezing & Lyophilization: Freeze the dialyzed polymer solution completely and lyophilize (freeze-dry) it to isolate the pure, water-soluble SMA copolymer as a dry powder.
  3. Prepare 5% (w/v) Polymer Solution in Buffer: Weigh 0.0075 g (7.50 mg) of the lyophilized SMA polymer and dissolve it in 150 μL (0.150 mL) of buffer to obtain a 5% (w/v) polymer solution.
  4. Prepare 100 mM POPC Stock from Solid Lipid: Weigh 0.0076 g (7.60 mg) of solid POPC lipid (MW 760.08 g/mol) and suspend/hydrate it in 100 μL (0.100 mL) of buffer to make a 100 mM POPC stock solution.
  5. Mix at 1 : 1.5 (Lipid : Polymer, v/v) Ratio: Mix 100 μL of the 100 mM POPC stock with 150 μL of the 5% SMA polymer solution for a 1 : 1.5 (v/v) lipid-to-polymer ratio (total volume 250 μL).
  6. Freeze–Thaw Cycles & DLS Size Check: Perform three (3) freeze–thaw cycles with gentle mixing until the suspension clarifies into nanodiscs, then analyze the sample on Dynamic Light Scattering (DLS) to check the hydrodynamic size of the nanodiscs.

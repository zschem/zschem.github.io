Core Competencies, Instrumentation & Documents

Comprehensive synthetic, formulation, biophysical, and analytical capabilities across polymer chemistry, lipid nanoparticles, and membrane protein characterization.

1. Liposomal, Lipid-Based & Polymeric Biomaterials

Design, hydration, and self-assembly of biomimetic phospholipid vesicles, hybrid polymer–lipid nanodiscs (3–60 nm), and functional macromolecular networks.

Liposome & Vesicle Formulation (POPC, POPG, DMPC) Polymer–Lipid Nanoparticles (VEMALPs & SMALPs) Programmable Sizing (3–60 nm) Rapid Aqueous Self-Assembly (<60 s VEMA) Scalable Thin-Film Nanoparticle Fabrication Polymeric Nanoparticles & Micelles Antiviral Bilayer-Disrupting Polymer Networks

2. Chemical Synthesis, Bioconjugation & Surface Functionalization

Controlled radical polymerization, small-molecule reagent synthesis, and modular post-polymerization bioconjugation for charge-tunable and stealth biomaterials.

RAFT Polymerization (Alternating & Block Copolymers) Small-Molecule CTA Synthesis (iBADTC, PADTC, EtPAX, PAETC) Nucleophilic Ring-Opening Bioconjugation PEGylation (Stealth Surface Engineering) Amino Acid Bioconjugation Cationic / Anionic / Zwitterionic Modification (VEMADs) Cross-Linked Hydrogel & Heavy-Metal Sorbent Networks

3. Nanoparticle Characterization & Analytical Toolkit

Multi-scale structural, morphological, spectroscopic, chromatographic, and mechanical characterization of polymers, liposomes, and protein–lipid assemblies.

Dynamic Light Scattering (DLS) Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM) Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) Size Exclusion / Gel Permeation Chromatography (SEC/GPC) 1D ¹H NMR & 2D DOSY NMR Continuous-Wave Electron Paramagnetic Resonance (CW-EPR) UV-Vis & FTIR Spectroscopy Rheometry & Instron Mechanical Testing DSC, ICP-OES & AAS Gamma Irradiation Radiolysis

4. Biological Interface & Membrane Protein Reconstitution

Detergent-free extraction, native-like reconstitution, and biophysical stability profiling of transmembrane proteins and peptides.

Proteoliposome & Nanodisc Reconstitution Human TRPV1 Ion Channel Stabilization KCNE1 & KCNE4 Membrane Protein Assays Bacteriophage gp28 Peptide Topology Site-Directed Spin Labeling (SDSL) CW-EPR Physiological pH Stability Profiling (pH 3.0–9.0) Divalent Cation Tolerance Assays (up to 30 mM Mg²⁺) Biomimetic Vesicle Remodeling Assays

5. Laboratory Leadership, Mentorship & Operations

Proven track record managing multi-investigator research workflows, safety compliance, and trainee development.

Multi-Investigator NIH & NSF Project Coordination Mentorship of 13 Researchers (11 Undergrad, 2 Grad) Chemical Inventory & Procurement Management Instrument Maintenance & Troubleshooting Safety & Experimental SOP Development Grant Progress Reporting & Manuscript Preparation

6. Computational, Modeling & Design Tools

Scientific data analysis, polymerization kinetic modeling, spectral processing, and 3D CAD fabrication.

Bruker TopSpin (1D/2D & DOSY NMR) KaleidaGraph Python (Kinetic Modeling & Data Visualization) MATLAB (Basics) Fiji / ImageJ (TEM/SEM Particle Analysis) Autodesk Fusion 360 (CAD & 3D Printing Fabrication) ChemDraw & Adobe Illustrator
